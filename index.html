About the Course

An 8‑week live cohort course designed for researchers and knowledge workers who want to apply Generative AI in a practical, structured way. The course combines conceptual grounding with hands‑on experimentation, focusing on no‑code tools to automate analysis, accelerate literature reviews, and improve scientific communication.

Module 1 — Websites, Documents & Presentations Expand
  • LLM fundamentals and how large language models generate text
  • Generating Markdown, LaTeX, and HTML documents
  • Building simple research websites with no‑code tools
  • AI‑assisted presentations and posters for scientific communication
Module 2 — AI‑Automated Data Analysis Expand
  • Using ChatGPT Code Interpreter for exploratory analysis
  • Cleaning and transforming datasets with AI tools
  • Automated visualizations and reporting workflows
  • Dashboards and recurring analysis pipelines
Module 3 — GenAI for Research & Text Analysis Expand
  • Efficient literature reviews with AI‑assisted tools
  • Document summarization and knowledge extraction
  • Text classification and sentiment analysis
  • Knowledge synthesis from large text collections
Module 4 — Custom GPTs & Chatbots Expand
  • Understanding LLM chat architecture and context windows
  • Building custom GPTs for specific research tasks
  • Retrieval‑augmented generation (RAG) systems
  • Interactive research assistants and chatbots
Module 5 — AI for Task Automation Expand
  • Designing AI‑driven automation workflows
  • Email and administrative task automation
  • Agent‑based systems for complex processes
  • Orchestrating multi‑step AI workflows
Module 6 — GenAI for Communication & Outreach Expand
  • AI‑generated research summaries for different audiences
  • Designing scientific figures and visuals with AI
  • AI‑assisted presentations and explanatory content
  • Strategies for research dissemination and outreach
Module 7 — Capstone Project & Showcase Expand
  • Scoping and designing a real‑world AI solution
  • Iterative development with peer and instructor feedback
  • Troubleshooting and refining AI workflows
  • Final presentation and project showcase
